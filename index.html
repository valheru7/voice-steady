Resonance (F2) while talking. Higher means a brighter, more forward sound. It's rougher than pitch and changes with your mic and with the vowel, so compare it with your own normal voice, not with a fixed number.

Saves only your mic (no game audio) as 5-minute files in the folder you pick, so voice analysis hears just you. About 10-15 MB per hour. If the tab crashes you lose at most the last 5 minutes. Needs Chrome or Edge.
